Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can visitors check in using QR pass? ▼
On average, a returning visitor or pre-registered guest checks in under 4 seconds by scanning their mobile QR code at the reception tablet.
Can we brand the kiosk and badge design? ▼
Yes, full white-labeling is supported. You can upload your company logo, set custom brand colors, custom NDAs, and badge layouts.
Is guest data compliant with GDPR and CCPA? ▼
Absolutely. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with automated data retention and purging policies per your legal requirements.
